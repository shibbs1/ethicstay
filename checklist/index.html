Before Every Guest Arrives

Cleaning checklist — Elizabeth & Jose Uncle
Clean the day before the guests come, or that same morning. Never more than one day before. If the house is cleaned early and then kept closed, the bathrooms start to smell again.
If something is broken, or will not come clean, phone Shibu at once — in the morning. After 6 PM no maid, plumber or electrician can be found in Changanassery until the next day.

1. Bathrooms All six · the most important part

  1. Open every tap and let the water run for one minute
  2. Flush every toilet twice
  3. Pour a mug of water into every floor drain When the house is closed the drains dry out. That is what causes the bad smell — even when the bathroom looks clean.
  4. Scrub the toilet, seat and floor with cleaning liquid and a brush Jet spray alone is not enough.
  5. After cleaning, smell the bathroom. If there is any smell, clean it again
  6. New soap, toilet paper and Odonil in every bathroom
  7. Wipe the floor dry

2. Bedrooms

  1. Fresh bedsheets and pillow covers on every bed
  2. Mosquito repellent in every bedroom — plugged in and switched ON
  3. Check that the fan and lights work
  4. AC remote in the room, with working batteries

3. Kitchen & Fridge

  1. Empty the fridge completely — throw away anything the last guests left
  2. Wipe the fridge clean inside
  3. Clean the gas stove and the counter
  4. Drinking water ready and in place

4. Whole House

  1. Sweep and mop both floors
  2. Sweep the front yard
  3. WiFi — check on your own phone that it connects
  4. Fill the water tank — set a 10-minute timer and do not forget the motor running

5. Last — Jose Uncle walks through

Before you both leave, Jose Uncle goes into each of the six bathrooms and looks:
  • No stains on the pot, the seat or the wash basin
  • No smell — stand inside and smell it, do not check from the door
  • Floor dry, no water logging
  • Soap, toilet paper and Odonil in place
Then look inside the fridge, and into each bedroom.
If anything is not right, Elizabeth does it again before she leaves. If it cannot be put right today, phone Shibu — before 6 PM.
Jose Uncle: two minutes in each bathroom is enough. You are not being asked to clean — only to look, and to say so if it is not right. Saying it is fine when it is not is what costs us the booking.
If it is a function booking (marriage or party), Saju also checks the house — usually the afternoon or evening before, while Elizabeth is finishing, so anything wrong is put right the same day. His list is at ethicstay.com/checklist/saju

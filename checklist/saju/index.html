Visit 1 — Before the guests arrive

Morning of arrival day, at the latest. Elizabeth cleans the day before.

Bathrooms — all six, the most important

  1. No stains on the pot, seat or wash basin — look closely, not from the door
  2. Smell each bathroom. If there is any smell, it is not done
  3. Run a tap and flush each toilet — check no smell comes back Dry drain traps are what caused the August complaint.
  4. Floor dry · no water logging in the common bathroom
  5. Soap, toilet paper and Odonil in place

Rooms & kitchen

  1. Fridge empty, wiped, no stains inside
  2. Fresh sheets and pillow covers on every bed
  3. Mosquito repellent in every bedroom — plugged in and switched ON
  4. Kitchen, gas stove and counters clean · drinking water ready
  5. Both floors swept and mopped · front yard swept

Working order

  1. WiFi — connect on your own phone and confirm it works
  2. Water tank full
  3. Fans, lights, AC remotes with batteries

Then

  1. Anything not right — get it fixed the same morning, or call Shibu at once
  2. Message Shibu "House ready" with photos of the bathrooms and the fridge
₹1,000

Visit 2 — At checkout

While the family is still there. Do not wait until they have left.
Walk the house with the family present. Once they have driven off, any damage becomes an argument on the phone. With them standing there it is a two-minute conversation.

Damage check

  1. All six bathrooms · both floors · every bedroom
  2. Tiles, walls, pillars and oodh — no drilling, nails or tape marks House freshly painted in 2026. Damages charged at actuals.
  3. TVs, ACs, fridge, fans, lights, water dispensers — all working
  4. Kitchen items and crockery against the showcase inventory
  5. Front yard clean · waste disposed — food to the ring pit, paper and leaves to the burn corner, plastic bagged

Settle it there and then

  1. Any damage — agree the cost with the family before they leave
  2. Tell Shibu what to deduct, or confirm the ₹5,000 deposit goes back in full

After they have gone

  1. Check the post-checkout clean was done properly — bedsheets washed, bathrooms, floors, waste cleared
  2. Report anything that needs repair while the house is empty Far cheaper to fix now than to have the next guest find it.
